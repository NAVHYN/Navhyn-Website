Our approach

Clarity before commitment.

Before an engagement begins, we aim to confirm what is included, who is responsible for each activity, how communication will work and which third-party providers may be involved. Any regulated or specialist work should be performed by appropriately qualified professionals where required.

Typical next steps

  • Initial discussion of your needs and objectives
  • Confirmation of scope, assumptions and responsibilities
  • Proposal covering delivery approach and commercial terms
  • Written agreement before services commence
Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Which roles can you help source?

Tell us the role, seniority, location and skills required. Feasibility depends on the available talent pool and the specific profile.

Will NAVHYN make the final hiring decision?

No. The intended approach is to support sourcing and screening while the client retains final selection and technical assessment.

Do you guarantee a placement date?

No specific placement date should be assumed until confirmed. Recruitment timelines depend on candidate availability, response times and the agreed selection process.
