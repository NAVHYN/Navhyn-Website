Our approach

Clarity before commitment.

Before an engagement begins, we aim to confirm what is included, who is responsible for each activity, how communication will work and which third-party providers may be involved. Any regulated or specialist work should be performed by appropriately qualified professionals where required.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What is included in a managed office service?

The scope can be tailored. It may combine dedicated personnel, employment/EOR coordination, workspace and local operational administration. All inclusions, exclusions and fees should be confirmed in a written proposal.

Does NAVHYN take over technical management of the client’s team?

The proposed model is designed to provide local operational support. The client should retain technical direction, work allocation and acceptance of technical deliverables unless a separate written arrangement states otherwise.

Can the service start with a small team?

The proposed model can be discussed for an initial small team, subject to staffing availability, location, facility capacity and agreed commercial terms.
