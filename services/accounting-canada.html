Our approach

Clarity before commitment.

Before an engagement begins, we aim to confirm what is included, who is responsible for each activity, how communication will work and which third-party providers may be involved. Any regulated or specialist work should be performed by appropriately qualified professionals where required.

Typical next steps

  • Initial discussion of your needs and objectives
  • Confirmation of scope, assumptions and responsibilities
  • Proposal covering delivery approach and commercial terms
  • Written agreement before services commence
Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is this service intended for?

The page is intended for small and medium-sized businesses operating in Canada that want to discuss accounting or bookkeeping support.

Does this page promise tax filing or CPA services?

No. The exact scope must be agreed. Tax filings, assurance and other regulated or reserved services should only be provided by appropriately qualified and authorized professionals where required.

Can the support work with our existing accountant?

Yes, that can be discussed. The scope may be designed to support your internal team or coordinate with your existing accountant, subject to access, privacy and professional requirements.
