Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What is an Employer of Record (EOR)?

An EOR is a third-party organization that legally employs a worker on behalf of a client business under an agreed arrangement. The exact allocation of responsibilities depends on the provider, contract and applicable law.

Does NAVHYN itself act as the legal employer in India?

NAVHYN’s proposed service model may involve coordinating with a local employment/EOR partner. The legal employer and each party’s responsibilities must be confirmed in writing for the specific engagement.

Can EOR remove every need for an Indian subsidiary?

Not necessarily. The appropriate structure depends on the activities being performed and the client’s legal, tax and regulatory circumstances. Clients should obtain independent professional advice before choosing a structure.
