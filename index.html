About NAVHYN

A managed local office for your engineering team.

NAVHYN is a Canadian business focused on helping engineering and design firms build and operate teams in India without taking on the local infrastructure themselves.

We support the practical side of the operation — helping with talent acquisition, workplace, employment administration, local coordination and cultural onboarding — while your team remains responsible for the engineering work and technical performance.

Our goal is simple: give your company a functioning local engineering office that can start small, fit into your existing way of working, and grow as your requirements grow.

How NAVHYN Works

One local operating layer around your engineering team.

You define the work, standards and technical requirements. NAVHYN helps put the local team and operating structure around it so your company can focus on managing engineering output.

STEP 01

Define

We understand the roles, technical requirements, working style and team structure you need.

STEP 02

Select

We source and screen candidates; you make the final technical hiring decision.

STEP 03

Set Up

We coordinate employment administration, office readiness and the local operating setup.

STEP 04

Operate

Your team works for you while NAVHYN supports local administration, employee concerns and coordination.

STEP 05

Scale

Add people as your workload grows without rebuilding the local operating layer.
